Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

Who we are

Stackly Technologies Pvt. Ltd., 14 Omalur Main Road, Fairlands, Salem 636016, runs this website and the courier service. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

Account details: name, email, mobile number and role.

Shipment details: sender and receiver names, addresses, pincodes, phone numbers, parcel weight and contents description.

Proof of delivery: OTP confirmation, signature and a doorstep photo.

Payment records: transaction IDs and amounts. We do not store card numbers.

How we use it

To pick up, move and deliver parcels; to send tracking SMS and emails; to settle cash on delivery; to handle claims; and to meet GST and legal record-keeping duties.

Who we share it with

Delivery partners on lanes we do not run ourselves, payment gateways, SMS providers and, for international parcels, customs authorities. We never sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Shipment records for 8 years for tax purposes. Doorstep photos for 90 days. Account data until you close your account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by writing to privacy@stackly.in. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies

This site stores your sign-in session in your browser only for the current tab. We do not use advertising cookies.

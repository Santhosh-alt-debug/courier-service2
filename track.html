Common tracking questions.

Tracking questions

My ID says not found. What now?

New bookings appear within 15 minutes. Check that the ID starts with SK and has 10 digits. If it still does not show, call +91 427 240 1100.

The status has not changed for a day.

Surface parcels between distant hubs can take 24–36 hours with no scan. If it is longer than that, raise a query from your dashboard.

Can I change the delivery address?

Yes, until the parcel is out for delivery. Call the desk with the tracking ID and the new address in the same city.

Can someone else receive my parcel?

Yes. Share the OTP from the delivery SMS with them. COD parcels must be paid before handover.

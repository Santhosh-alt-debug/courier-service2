About the price

Why does the price change when I enter box size?

Large light boxes take up truck space. We use volumetric weight (L × W × H ÷ 5000) when it is higher than the actual weight.

Is GST included?

The calculator shows GST at 18% as its own line. Business accounts get a GST invoice with your GSTIN.

Is there a fuel surcharge?

Yes, 12% on base freight. We review it on the first of each month in line with diesel prices.

Do you charge for pickup?

No. Pickup from your door is free on every service.

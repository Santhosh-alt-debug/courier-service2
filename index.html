Questions

Quick answers before you book.

Good to know before you ship

How late can I book a same-day pickup?

Book by 11 am for delivery the same evening within your region. Bookings after 11 am go out first thing the next morning.

What if nobody is home?

The courier calls you and the receiver. We make two more attempts on the next working days at no extra charge before returning the parcel.

Can I pay the courier in cash?

Yes. You can pay online, by UPI at pickup, or in cash at any Stackly counter. Business accounts are billed monthly.

Do you deliver to PO boxes?

No. We need a street address and a phone number so the courier can reach the receiver.

How do I raise a claim for a damaged parcel?

Raise it from your dashboard within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the box and contents. Insured parcels are settled within 7 working days.

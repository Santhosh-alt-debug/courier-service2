Our story

How a four-bike counter became a national network.

A courier built on routes we know

Stackly began in 2014 when local weavers in Salem needed a dependable way to send sarees to buyers in Chennai and Bengaluru. We learned every lane of the city first, then every highway out of it.

Today the same rule holds: a parcel is never out of sight. It is scanned at every handover, and you can see each scan.

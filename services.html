Prohibited items

Some things we cannot carry, by law or for everyone's safety.

What we cannot carry

If you are unsure, call the Salem desk before booking. Parcels with prohibited items are held at the hub and returned at the sender's cost.

Ask the desk
  • Cash, coins and bearer cheques
  • Gold, silver and loose gemstones
  • Firearms, ammunition and replicas
  • Explosives, fireworks and gas cylinders
  • Flammable liquids, paints and solvents
  • Narcotics and prescription drugs without a bill
  • Live animals and plants
  • Perishable food that is not sealed
